Privacy Policy for Ship One / Ship Jam

Effective Date: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Ship One (also referred to as Ship Jam, developed by SeeOrbit) collects, uses, and shares information when you play our mobile game application.

1. Information We Collect

Ship One is an offline-capable casual harbor puzzle game. We do NOT collect, store, or require personal identifying information such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.

All in-game progress (such as unlocked levels, coin balances, stars earned, and fleet customization preferences) is stored locally on your device via local persistent storage and is never uploaded to any personal database.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To provide this game as a free-to-play experience, we use Google AdMob to display Banner, Interstitial, and user-initiated Rewarded Video ads. Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain device information, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID), Android ID, or other persistent device identifiers.
  • Approximate Location: Country or city level location derived from your IP address (used to deliver relevant ads).
  • Ad Interaction Telemetry: Ad impressions, click activity, and general app interaction performance.
  • Diagnostics: Crash logs, application performance metrics, and network connection status.

For more information on how Google handles and protects your data, please review Google's official privacy policy: Google Advertising Privacy & Terms.

3. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our game is intended for general audiences aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal data through a third-party ad interaction, please contact us immediately, and we will take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards or deletion.

4. In-App Ads & Rewarded Ads Policy

All Rewarded Video Ads in Ship One are entirely optional and user-initiated. Players may choose to watch a video ad in exchange for in-game virtual goods (such as bonus coins, free move undos, power-up refills, or level revives). There are no forced paywalls, and rewards do not constitute real currency.

5. Data Security

Data transmitted between your device and Google AdMob ad servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Secure Sockets Layer (SSL / TLS) HTTPS encryption protocols.

6. Your Data Rights & Opt-Out

You can reset your device's Advertising ID or opt out of personalized interest-based advertising at any time through your Android device settings:

Settings > Google > Ads > Reset Advertising ID or Delete Advertising ID

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will become effective immediately upon posting the revised policy on this page.
