# Privacy Policy for Ship One / Ship Jam

**Effective Date:** November 10, 2026  
**Developer:** CozyBitsKsr  
**Contact:** cozybitsksr@gmail.com  

This Privacy Policy describes how **Ship One** (also known as **Ship Jam**) collects, uses, and shares data when you play our mobile game.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
Ship One is an offline-capable casual puzzle game. We do **not** collect or store personal identifying data such as names, phone numbers, or email addresses.  
All game progression (levels cleared, coin count, high scores, skins) is saved locally on your device via local storage.

---

### 2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)
We use **Google AdMob** to display Banner, Interstitial, and user-initiated Rewarded Video ads to keep the game 100% free to play.  
Google AdMob may collect:
* **Device Identifiers:** Google Advertising ID (AAID), Android ID.
* **Approximate Location:** IP-based country or region.
* **Ad Analytics:** Ad impressions, clicks, and diagnostic telemetry.

Google's privacy policy can be viewed at: [Google Advertising & Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

---

### 3. Children's Privacy (COPPA)
Our application is designed for general audiences aged **13 and older**. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 years of age.

---

### 4. Rewarded Ads & Virtual Currency
All rewarded video ads are **strictly opt-in** and initiated by the player. Rewards consist exclusively of in-game virtual currency (coins) and consumable gameplay items (undo moves, power-up refills, level revives). Rewards hold no real-world monetary value.

---

### 5. Security & Opt-Out
All data transmissions with ad networks are encrypted in transit via HTTPS (SSL/TLS).  
You may reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time via Android device settings:  
`Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or Delete Advertising ID`.
